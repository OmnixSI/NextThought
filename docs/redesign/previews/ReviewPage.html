3
如果删除缓存失败了怎么办？What if deleting the cache fails?[18:22]答得好Strong
我答：重试队列 + binlog 异步删除，两道保险。I said: retry queue + async delete via binlog, two layers.
5
热点 key 过期的一瞬间大量请求打到数据库，怎么办？A hot key expires and traffic floods the DB — what now?[27:05]待改进Improve
我答：加锁……（停顿 8 秒）……可能用布隆过滤器。I said: a lock… (8-second pause) …maybe a Bloom filter.
更好的答法A BETTER ANSWER
先给结论：互斥锁重建 + 逻辑过期，二选一看一致性要求。布隆过滤器解决的是穿透，不是击穿，别混用。Lead with it: mutex rebuild or logical expiry, picked by consistency needs. Bloom filters solve penetration, not breakdown — don't mix them.
